Turn a floor plan into 3D

Upload a plan as an image or PDF. The app finds the walls, doors, windows and rooms, lets you fix anything it missed, then builds a 3D model you can tour.

Drop a floor plan here, or click to choose PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or PDF

1 · UploadPlans with solid dark walls on a light background work best. Scans and photos are fine if the walls are clear.
2 · CheckCorrect walls, doors, windows and rooms, set the scale, name the rooms and add furniture.
3 · ExploreTour the 3D model with cutaway, night lights, labels and the plan overlay.

Before you upload

Detection reads thick dark walls. If your plan draws walls as two thin lines, tick Walls drawn as outlines in the next step and press Re-detect.

Everything runs in your browser. Your plan is not uploaded anywhere.
